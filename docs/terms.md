# Terms

A summary, not legal advice: read the Bank's own words before relying on
them. Terms change; these were read on 19 November 2026.

From the Bank of England's legal page
([bankofengland.co.uk/legal](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/legal)) and
its yield curves page
([bankofengland.co.uk/statistics/yield-curves](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/statistics/yield-curves)):

- **The Database.** Its data are the copyright of the Governor and Company
  of the Bank of England; reproducing them is subject to the UK Open
  Government Licence.
- **SONIA.** The statement its use requires: "SONIA and/or SONIA Compounded
  Index data licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0 and copyright
  the Governor and Company of the Bank of England." "Bank of England" and
  "SONIA" are registered trade marks of the Bank of England.
- **Exchange rates.** Selected exchange rate data and series are excluded
  from the licence, reproduced by the Bank under licence from third parties
  (LSEG's). The Bank states its rates are not official rates.
- **Yield curves.** Their sources are Bloomberg Finance L.P., Tradeweb (the
  gilt curve) and Bank calculations. The Bank sees no objection to their
  electronic storage used under its copyright and disclaimer terms; they are
  revised, the latest on its website.
- **Excessive use.** The Bank may restrict a user's access for it.

**Served, not kept.** The exchange rates (`boe_fx`) and the curves
(`boe_curves`) are read only when your lake wants a day, and the plugin
keeps none of their answers. Each one's terms carry the clause, and "served,
not kept" is the licence default it asks you to set.

Each dataset describes these terms in words, shown on the Data sources
page, and declares no licence. The licence a deployment admin sets there is
the one enforced; the plugin says nothing of whether your deployment meets
anyone's terms.
